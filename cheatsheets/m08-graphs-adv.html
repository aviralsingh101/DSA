Bellman-Ford

  • relax all edges n−1 times
  • one more success → neg cycle

Floyd

for k for i for j
  d[i][j]=min(d[i][j], d[i][k]+d[k][j])

Kruskal

sort edges by w
if(dsu.union(u,v)) take

Tarjan low

low[v]=min(low[v],low[to])
low[to]>tin[v] → bridge
low[to]>=tin[v] → articulation (not root)

Dinic idea

  • BFS level graph
  • DFS blocking flow along levels
  • O(V²E) typical

2-SAT

  • implication graph x∨y ⇒ ¬x→y and ¬y→x
  • unsat iff x and ¬x in same SCC
  • assign x true if scc[x]>scc[¬x]
